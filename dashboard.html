📌 The problem

The gateway reports 41,208 settled transactions for June 2025; the finance ledger reports 41,193. Netting the two suggests a gap of just 15 — but that single number hides 208 transactions the ledger never saw, 193 the gateway lacks, and 450 more that both systems hold yet disagree on. This project replaces that misleading net figure with a full, reasoned break list built on Databricks and served through Snowflake.

🏗️ Pipeline architecture

Two systems stay apart until Silver makes an explicit, documented matching decision — nothing is unioned or cleaned in Bronze.

🔍 Key findings

  1. Untrimmed keys create 75 false breaks per side (283/268 instead of the true 208/193).
  2. 350 gateway amounts are unreadable ('NA') — logged and kept, not dropped.
  3. 210 ledger amounts use comma formatting (e.g. 12,450.00) and need stripping before try_cast.
  4. 400 of the 450 mismatches are recurring fee deductions (~1.77%), not real errors.
  5. A FULL OUTER JOIN is required — INNER hides 401 rows, LEFT hides all 193 ledger-only rows.
